VIBEPOD

Create with AI on the go.

Vibepod puts all your hotkeys and a built-in microphone in one device. It's made for working professionals and students to vibe-code their projects and get tasks done with any major AI agent.

Three mechanical keys

Real keys, any action.

Each key can type a letter, fire a shortcut, run a macro of several steps, or control your media. Full-travel mechanical switches give every press a clear click.

Side scroll wheel

Turn it for anything with a range.

Volume, zoom, brush size, timeline scrubbing. The wheel sits under your thumb on the side, so your fingers stay on the keys.
